# Support

Atap is free software, maintained by one person. Replies come when time allows.

## Get help

- **Help pages:** [atap.novafortress.my/help](https://atap.novafortress.my/help) covers setup and common questions.
- **Questions and ideas:** ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/anake-an/atap/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/anake-an/atap/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include your Atap version, your Windows version and the steps to reproduce it.
- **A feature you want:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/anake-an/atap/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) or a [widget request](https://github.com/anake-an/atap/issues/new?template=widget_request.yml).

## Find your logs

Logs are in `%APPDATA%\Atap\logs`, one file a day. Check the file for anything private before you share it. Atap leaves out song titles and replaces your user folder name, but read it first.

## Security problems

Do not report a vulnerability in a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) to report it privately.

## Conduct

To report a problem with someone's behaviour, see [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
