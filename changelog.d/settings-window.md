### Added

- Settings. Open it from the notch menu, or start Atap again while it runs. It has pages for Home, What it shows, Prayer times, Privacy and About, plus an Ask AI page for a later update.
- Home has Show Atap, Start with Windows, the language and where the notch sits. Pick one of five spots from a picture, and choose which screen shows it.
- What it shows lists everything on your notch and how each one is doing. A part that stopped after a problem has a Restart button, and each part has its own settings.
- Privacy lists what uses the internet, and whether it is on.
- About shows your version, with Check for updates, and the licences.
- Start with Windows. It is off until you turn it on. It stays on across updates, and uninstalling Atap removes it. The Microsoft Store version does not have it yet.
- Show Atap. Turn it off to hide the notch until you turn it on again.
- Settings work with the keyboard and screen readers, in English and Bahasa Melayu.
- Settings that are not valid show a short message under the field and keep the last good value.

### Security

- Keys and passwords typed into Settings are kept with Windows data protection on this PC. The settings files only hold a reference to them.
