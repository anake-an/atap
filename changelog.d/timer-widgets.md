### Added

- Focus timer widget: focus sessions with short and long breaks. Start, pause, add 5 minutes, skip or start over from the notch.
- The notch shows the phase and the minutes left while the focus timer runs. An alert says when a session or break ends.
- Stopwatch widget with laps.
- World clock widget: the time in up to three places, with how far ahead or behind they are. London, Tokyo and Riyadh to start.
- The focus timer and stopwatch keep going if Atap or the PC restarts. A session that ended while Atap was closed is reported once when it opens.
- Timers only tick every second while you have them open, and once a minute in the small notch. Hidden, they wake only when a session ends.
