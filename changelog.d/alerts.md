### Added

- Alerts show on the notch as a short pill, then the notch goes back to what it showed before.
- More urgent alerts go first. An alarm stays until you close it, for up to a minute.
- Alerts that come during a game, a full-screen video or Do not disturb wait in the new Missed alerts widget. Alarms still show as soon as the notch is back, and their sound plays on time.
- An Alert sound setting: the Windows sound, your own sound file or no sound. A sound plays once, never repeats, and the Stop button on the alert ends it.
- A "Stay quiet during Do not disturb" setting, on by default.
- Commands to stop the alert sound and to clear missed alerts.
- Scripts that send alerts to Atap can send 3 at once, then 1 every 5 seconds.
