### Added

- Next class widget: your next class from your timetable, with its time, room and a countdown. The small notch shows it from 15 minutes before it starts until it ends.
- Assignments widget: your deadlines, nearest first, with "3 days left" or "3 hari lagi". Tick one off when it is done.
- A new card in Settings, on the What it shows page, to add your timetable and assignments.

### Fixed

- To-do: the day buttons are now rounded rectangles, and the done tick is a clear check mark.
- Alarm buttons, the waktu solat Ramadan badge and the class room tag are rounded rectangles too, not ovals.
