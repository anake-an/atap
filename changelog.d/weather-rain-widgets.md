### Added

- Weather widget: MET Malaysia's forecast for your state, with today in full and the next three days. It updates every 3 hours and shows the last forecast after a restart.
- Rain alerts widget: thunderstorm and heavy rain warnings for your state, as an alert on the notch. Each warning alerts once, even after a restart.
- Rain alerts fetch the whole warning list and pick your state on your PC, so your state is never sent. They work even when the card is not on any page.
- Both use the state you pick on the Prayer times page.
