### Added

- Waktu solat widget: the next prayer and a countdown on the notch, and every time for today when you open it. Times come from JAKIM's e-Solat, with api.waktusolat.app as a backup.
- The notch shows the next prayer 10 minutes before it, and a short alert at the prayer time. Both can be changed or turned off.
- During Ramadan the widget also counts down to imsak and to berbuka.
- Hijri date widget, with JAKIM's official date for your area.
- A new card on the Prayer times page in Settings: pick your state and area, or press Find my area. Your location stays on your PC.
- On the left or right edge, the idle clock shows the hours above the minutes.

### Privacy

- Nothing is fetched until you pick an area. Then Atap asks e-Solat for one month of times about once a month, and keeps them on your PC.
- Old data never gives an alert. When the times are out of date, the widget says so.
