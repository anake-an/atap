### Added

- Alarms widget: set alarms on the notch with hour and minute buttons, and pick the days they repeat. With no days picked, an alarm rings once.
- An alarm rings with an alert and sound, and you can snooze it for 5 minutes from the card. It rings even when the card is not on any page.
- An alarm whose time passed while Atap was closed or the PC slept shows "Missed alarm" once.
- Water widget: a gentle reminder to drink water during the hours you pick, and a count of glasses for the day.
- Water reminders wait while the PC is locked, and start counting again when you unlock it.
