### Added

- Battery widget: how much is left and whether it is charging. When you plug in, the small notch says "Charging" for 10 seconds.
- A battery low alert, once each time the battery runs down. You pick the level, or turn it off.
- On a PC without a battery, the widget says so and stays quiet.
- Wi-Fi widget: whether you are connected, the signal strength, and whether the connection is metered.
- The Wi-Fi name shows only if you turn that on. Windows may ask for Location, and the name stays on your PC.
- The Wi-Fi signal is read only while the card is open, every 30 seconds.
