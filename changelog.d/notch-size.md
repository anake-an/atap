### Added

- Notch size, on the Home page of Settings and in the notch's right-click menu. Slim is a thin bar that opens when you point at it, and music and alerts still show. Tucked away is a line at the screen edge, and only urgent alerts drop down. A short coloured line on the bar shows when something is playing.
- Hide for 1 hour, in the right-click menu. The notch comes back by itself after an hour, also if the PC slept in between, or straight away when you start Atap again.

### Fixed

- The notch now resizes when a new alert replaces the one on screen, so a longer alert is never cut off.
