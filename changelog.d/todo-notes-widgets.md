### Added

- To-do widget: add tasks in the notch panel and press Enter. Tick them off, give them a day (today or tomorrow), or remove them.
- A task with a day gets a reminder at 9 AM that day. Only the next reminder wakes Atap. Reminders that came while Atap was closed show once, and long-missed ones as one summary.
- Notes widget: write quick notes in the notch panel. They save a second after you stop typing, and at once when the panel closes. Pin a note to keep it at the top.
